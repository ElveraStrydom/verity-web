4. Emergencies

Verity is not for emergencies and is not an emergency service.

If you think you are having a medical emergency, or you might harm yourself or someone else, contact your local emergency services or a healthcare professional immediately. Use a real person who can help.

If a live Ask Vera question looks like a crisis, a request for a medication dose, or otherwise unsafe, our server stops it. The question never reaches the AI model, is not stored as a conversation, and does not use up a question. You may see in-chat crisis guidance. That guidance is information only. It is not treatment, and it is not a replacement for calling for help.

Browsing the ready answers is not a crisis screen. Do not wait on Verity if you need urgent help.

5. Vera — two different things

Vera has two surfaces. They are not the same, and they must not be described as the same.

A. Ready answers (Ask Vera)

You tap a question. You get a pre-written answer from a cited evidence library, served on your device. There is no AI model in this path. The answer does not need a network call.

Those articles are general educational information from cited sources. A clinician review is underway. They are not currently clinician-signed in the production app. They are not medical advice, not a diagnosis, and not a prescription.

You do not need an account to read them. They do not use the monthly live-question allowance.

Every answer you should treat as general information to discuss with your own clinician.

B. Live Ask Vera (optional, uses AI)

Live Ask Vera is on. You type a question in your own words.

  • The app sends the question to the Verity API in the EU, which sends it to OpenRouter (a third-party AI service, typically on US infrastructure) so a language model can draft an answer. We use OpenRouter’s Zero Data Retention setting and we block providers that train on prompts. We do not have a signed data-processing agreement with OpenRouter. Live Ask Vera is not medical advice and is not a medical device.
  • A little profile context goes with it (age, life stage, HRT status, recent symptoms, how you have been eating) — not your name or email.
  • You need an account.
  • Extra consent ticks appear before your first live question: that Vera is AI, not a doctor, and can be wrong; and that the question is sent to our AI provider. None of those ticks is pre-ticked.
  • Monthly allowance (calendar month, counted in UTC): 15 live questions. If you hit the wall mid-conversation, you may get up to five extra replies in the thread that is already open. That grace does not start a new thread and does not roll into next month.
  • There is no paid plan and no in-app purchase.
  • Ready Q&A taps do not count against the allowance. Crisis, dose and uncovered questions that never reach the model do not count. A failed call must not invent a fake answer; you should see that Vera is unavailable.
  • Live answers are AI-generated. They can be wrong. They are not clinician-reviewed. They may miss your history, your medicines, or the latest guideline for you.

Do not rely on Vera — either surface — as your doctor.

6. Support person

Support-person mode lets you invite someone you trust (a partner, a sister, a friend) to note how you seemed.

How it works

  • You send a one-time code (it lasts about three days). They install Verity and enter the code. They can start without making their own email login; they still have to accept the same kind of 18+, privacy and terms boxes you did.
  • Default: they see nothing of your health record. You opt in by category. The categories are: symptoms, sleep and energy, cycle, and HRT.
  • They can add notes you can read. Those notes can appear on your Home, in trends, and on your doctor report.
  • Vera chats, the health-background survey, and documents are not shared. There is no switch for them.
  • Either of you can end the link. Ending it stops further sharing and stops new notes.

After a revoke

  • Notes already written about you stay in your record.
  • You can delete a note. You cannot edit a note someone else wrote.
  • If you delete your account, notes about you go with your record.
  • If they delete their account, the notes they wrote about you are removed too.

Only invite someone you trust. Do not use this mode to monitor, coerce, or harm anyone. Doing so is a serious misuse of Verity and may be unlawful. We may end access if we believe it is being used that way.

Observations are personal information about the person they describe. They are handled as described in the Privacy Policy.
