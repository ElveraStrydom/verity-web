5. Emergencies

Verity is not an emergency service.

If you think you're having a medical emergency, or you might harm yourself or someone else, contact your local emergency number or a healthcare professional now.

If a live Vera question looks like a crisis, a request for a medicine dose, or otherwise unsafe, our server is built to stop it before it reaches the AI. A stopped question isn't stored as a conversation and doesn't use up a question. No check is perfect, so please never wait for Vera in an emergency. You may see crisis guidance in the chat. That guidance is information only. It isn't treatment, and it isn't a replacement for calling for help.

6. Vera: two different things

A. Ready answers (Ask Vera)

You tap a question and get a pre-written answer from our library of answers, each with its sources. These answers are stored on your phone. No AI is involved, and you don't need an account. They don't use any of your live questions.

A clinician hasn't reviewed these answers yet. They are general information to discuss with your own doctor, not medical advice.

B. Live Vera (uses AI)

You type a question in your own words, and an AI writes the answer.

  • You need an account, and you tick extra boxes before your first live question: that Vera is AI, not a doctor, and can be wrong, and that your question goes to our AI provider. None of these boxes is ticked for you.
  • Your question and a little context about you go to our AI provider. The Privacy Policy says exactly what is sent, to whom and where.
  • Live answers are written by AI. They can be wrong. A clinician hasn't reviewed them. They may miss your history, your medicines, or the latest guidance for you.
  • If live Vera can't answer, you'll see that she's unavailable, rather than a stand-in answer.

Monthly questions: everyone gets 15 live questions a calendar month at no cost. If you reach the limit in the middle of a conversation, you can get up to five more replies in that conversation. Verity Plus members get more (section 7). Unused questions don't carry over to the next month.

Please don't rely on Vera, in either form, as your doctor.

8. Support person

Support person mode lets you invite someone you trust, such as a partner, a sister or a friend, to share how you seemed.

How it works

  • You send a one-time code that lasts about three days. They install Verity, enter the code, and tick the same kind of 18+, privacy and terms boxes you did.
  • At first they see nothing of your record. You choose, one category at a time, what they see: symptoms, sleep and energy, cycle, and HRT.
  • They can add notes you can read. Those notes can appear on your Home screen, in your trends and, if you choose, on your doctor report.
  • Your Vera chats, your health profile and your documents are never shared. There is no switch for them.
  • Either of you can end the link at any time. That stops further sharing and new notes.

After the link ends

  • Notes already written about you stay in your record. You can delete a note. You can't edit a note someone else wrote.
  • If you delete your account, notes about you go with it.
  • If they delete their account, the notes they wrote about you are removed too.

Please only invite someone you trust. Using support person mode to watch, pressure or harm anyone is a serious misuse of Verity and may be against the law. We may end access if we believe it's being used that way.
