Delete your Verity account

This page explains how to delete your account in Verity (Verity Woman in the App Store, and in Google Play), the app run by Verity Women (Pty) Ltd, a company registered in South Africa (2026/890689/07).

You can delete your account in the app, or ask us by email if you no longer have the app.

Delete in the app (quickest)

  1. Open Verity.
  2. Go to Me, then Privacy & data.
  3. Tap Delete all my data.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete everything.

Your account is usually deleted straight away, and always within 30 days. Want a copy first? In Me → Privacy & data, export your data before you delete.

No longer have the app? Ask us by email

Email privacy@veritywomen.com from the email address your Verity account uses, with the subject Delete my account.

  • We check the request comes from that address before we delete anything, so no one else can delete your account.
  • If you signed in with Apple's Hide My Email, your account uses a hidden email address Apple made for you, which you may not know. Tell us in your email, and we'll help you confirm it's you.
  • Can't use that email address any more? Email us anyway, and we'll find another way to check it's you.
  • We delete your account within 30 days, and email you when it's done.

What is deleted

  • your account and login details;
  • your profile;
  • your logs, HRT records and notes;
  • your Vera conversations, any answers you reported, and, if you used Vera who remembers you, the log summaries kept for it;
  • your support-person link, and the notes written about you;
  • your subscription status and consent records;
  • the Verity data on the phone you delete from.

This can't be undone. It's a full delete, not a pause. If you have been someone's support person, the notes you wrote about them are removed from their record too.

If you signed in with Apple, deleting your account also asks Apple to end Verity's access to your Apple sign-in. If Apple's system doesn't answer, we still delete your Verity account.

What is not deleted, and for how long

  • Questions and log summaries already sent to our AI provider: Anthropic, which makes the AI model, deletes them automatically within 30 days. If its safety systems flag a message as breaking its usage rules, it may keep that message for up to 2 years, and longer where the law requires or to deal with rule-breaking. We don't attach your name or email; what you typed in a question is sent as you wrote it.
  • Usage data without your name (only if you turned on Help improve Verity): it isn't linked to your name or account, so we can't pick out yours. Our copy is deleted automatically after 90 days. Google keeps its copy, tied to a random app number, under its own settings.
  • Payments: Verity takes no payments yet.
  • Feedback you sent from the app (Tell us what you think): it isn't linked to your account, but it shows your email address if you typed one. It is deleted from our support mailbox after 12 months.
  • Emails you sent us, including a request to delete, are kept for 12 months so we can show we did what you asked.
  • Our database: we keep no backups at the moment, so nothing lingers there.
  • Doctor reports and exports you shared stay wherever you sent or saved them.
  • Phone backups (such as iCloud or Google backup) may keep a copy until that backup is replaced, under that provider's rules.

Used Verity without an account?

Then everything stays on your phone, and we hold nothing on our server. To delete it, go to Me → Privacy & data in the app, or uninstall Verity.
