Get help now

If you are in danger now, call your emergency number.

If you're thinking about harming yourself, please call now too. You don't need to be sure it's "serious enough". If you're worried about your safety, that's reason enough to call.

If you're not in immediate danger but you're struggling, you can talk to someone at one of the crisis lines below. Most are open day and night. You don't have to explain everything, and you don't have to have the right words.

Verity is not an emergency service, and we can't respond to a crisis. If you need help right now, please use the numbers on this page.

Not in this list?

Please remember

Verity is not an emergency service and can't help in a crisis. These services are run by other organisations, and their numbers and hours can change. If a number doesn't connect, please call your emergency number.

You deserve support, and reaching out is a strong thing to do.
